\subsection{US-02 --- Order Food}
\noindent\textbf{Role:} Customer\\
\textbf{Priority:} Must Have

\subsubsection*{User Story}
As a customer, I want to order food, so that I can have meals delivered to my chosen location.

\subsubsection*{Functional Acceptance Criteria}
\begin{itemize}
  \item $\square$ Can the customer select available dishes and specify their quantities?
  \item $\square$ Can the customer provide a delivery address and contact information?
  \item $\square$ Does the system validate required order information before processing payment?
  \item $\square$ Does the system validate the payment before confirming the order?
  \item $\square$ Does the customer receive an order confirmation after successful payment?
  \item $\square$ Does the system prevent confirmation when payment fails or the selected items are unavailable?
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{Non-Functional Requirements}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Security:} Payment information must be transmitted over an encrypted HTTPS connection.
  \item \textbf{Performance:} Order validation should complete within 2 seconds for at least 95\% of requests, excluding external payment processing.
  \item \textbf{Reliability:} A successfully paid order must not be created more than once if the customer repeats a request.
  \item \textbf{Privacy:} Customer contact information and delivery addresses must only be accessible to authorized users and services.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{Notes and Edge Cases}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The customer submits an empty order.
  \item The delivery address or contact information is incomplete.
  \item A selected dish becomes unavailable before payment.
  \item Payment is declined or the payment service times out.
  \item The customer submits the same order request multiple times.
\end{itemize}
