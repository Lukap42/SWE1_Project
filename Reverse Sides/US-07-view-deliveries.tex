\subsection{US-07 --- View Assigned Deliveries}
\noindent\textbf{Role:} Delivery Driver\\
\textbf{Priority:} Must Have

\subsubsection*{User Story}
As a delivery driver, I want to view my assigned deliveries, so that I know which orders I need to deliver and where.

\subsubsection*{Functional Acceptance Criteria}
\begin{itemize}
  \item $\square$ Can the delivery driver view a list of deliveries currently assigned to them?
  \item $\square$ Does each assigned delivery display its delivery address?
  \item $\square$ Can the driver identify which order corresponds to each delivery?
  \item $\square$ Does the system prevent a driver from viewing deliveries assigned to other drivers?
  \item $\square$ Does the delivery list reflect newly assigned orders after its data is refreshed?
  \item $\square$ Does the system display an appropriate message when no deliveries are assigned?
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{Non-Functional Requirements}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Performance:} The assigned deliveries list should load within 2 seconds for at least 95\% of requests under normal operating conditions.
  \item \textbf{Security:} Drivers must only access delivery information for orders currently assigned to them.
  \item \textbf{Usability:} The delivery list must remain readable and usable on mobile devices.
  \item \textbf{Reliability:} Successfully saved assignments must remain available after the driver signs out and signs in again.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{Notes and Edge Cases}
\begin{itemize}
  \item No deliveries are assigned to the driver.
  \item A new delivery is assigned while the driver is viewing the list.
  \item A delivery is reassigned to another driver.
  \item The driver temporarily loses network connectivity.
  \item The driver attempts to access another driver's delivery details.
\end{itemize}
